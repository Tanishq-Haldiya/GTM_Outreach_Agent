HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS

From a small clue to a grounded conversation.

We combine flexible target inputs with diverse public evidence, qualify what the evidence can support, and prepare a tailored outreach draft for a person to review. The system never sends outreach automatically.

THE PIPELINE

Four steps from input to review

Each stage narrows uncertainty and carries the supporting evidence forward.

01
RESOLVE

Start with what you know

Enter a company, a person, or both. A role helps shape buyer framing; optional identity clues can clarify a company or person. If identity confidence is low, the system asks for clarification.

CompanyPersonRoleOptional clues
02
RESEARCH

Search through different evidence lanes

Researchers gather company context, customer and employee voice, hiring, incidents, changelogs, regulatory records, news and public leadership commentary.

Parallel channelsPublic sourcesPerson voice when relevant
03
GROUND & RANK

Separate observed issues from hypotheses

Evidence is checked against the target profile. Problems are classified as direct or inferred, tied to supporting evidence, reviewed for grounding and ranked for relevance.

Entity relevanceGrounding GuardDirect vs. inferred
04
DESIGN & REVIEW

Prepare a draft, then leave the decision to a person

The selected opportunity informs a workflow concept and tailored outreach. Final checks flag unsupported claims and name-swap risk; a human edits, approves or rejects.

NEXT ITERATIONS

Future scope for improvement

Potential directions to improve evidence quality, reliability and learning from real usage.

01 · EVIDENCE QUALITY

Deduplicate and track freshness

Group repeated claims across pages, preserve publication dates, and make source freshness clearer in the evidence trail.

02 · LEARNING LOOP

Use reviewer decisions as feedback

Analyze edits, approvals and rejections to calibrate problem ranking and the confidence required for inferred opportunities.

03 · RATE-AWARE RUNS

Improve quota planning and resume

Track provider limits across minute and daily windows, queue work safely, and resume incomplete stages without repeating successful calls.

04 · EVALUATION

Benchmark the case library

Turn demo scenarios into repeatable evaluations for identity resolution, evidence coverage, grounding and draft relevance.

05 · SOURCE COVERAGE

Expand region-specific evidence

Add carefully reviewed local public records and source integrations while preserving provenance and relevance checks.

06 · HISTORY & OPERATIONS

Strengthen durable run history

Use persistent storage and retention controls so evidence, QA outcomes and reviewer decisions remain available across restarts.
